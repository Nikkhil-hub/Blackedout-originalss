01 / The organization

Not just a name.
A legacy.

BlackedOut Originals is a roleplay gang built on brotherhood. From the first meeting in IURS City to the 2026 reunion in TCRS by VIBE CITY, our identity has been shaped by loyalty, teamwork, business, and shared memories.

This site tells our history, showcases our crew and memories, and introduces the next chapter. City rules and staff decisions remain with the roleplay community we play in.

02 / The history behind the name

Different cities.
One brotherhood.

From the first meeting in IURS City to the 2026 reunion in TCRS by VIBE CITY, the BlackedOut story is built on brotherhood, loyalty, business, and memories made together across different roleplay cities.

CH. 01
Chapter 01 / The beginning

IURS City — Where brothers met

It began in Indian Ultra Roleplay city (IURS) City. A group of players met, built real friendships through roleplay, and became brothers. Before there was a gang name, there was a family.

CH. 02
Chapter 02 / The name is born

TCRP City — BlackedOut Originals

The family moved together to Telugu Community Roleplay (TCRP) City. In time, that brotherhood became a gang: BlackedOut Originals.

CH. 03
Chapter 03 / Old blood, new blood

Maayalokam City — A stronger family

As time passed, some founders and members became inactive. The family stepped into Maayalokam Roleplay City, where familiar faces met new blood and the gang entered another phase of growth.

MILESTONE
Chapter 04 / The first major venture

LS Customs acquired

BlackedOut successfully acquired LS Customs, marking the family's first major business venture. The gang expanded beyond its reputation and began building its presence through in-city business.

  • LS Customs: mechanic business, vehicle customization, and repair operations
  • Food business: food production, food sales, and business expansion
CH. 05
Chapter 05 / A quieter chapter

After the wars

BlackedOut faced rival gangs and police through intense roleplay conflicts. After the wars, the gang kept a lower profile for a while. Many members returned to everyday in-city life, while some moved on to a new city.

CH. 06
Chapter 06 / The journey continues

ATRP City — Another chapter

Some brothers continued their journey in Amazing Telugu Roleplay (ATRP) City. BlackedOut began another chapter, carrying its identity forward through business, teamwork, and the bonds between its members.

  • LS Customs: vehicle customization and repairs
  • Food business: food production, sales, and expansion
  • Family continued to grow through shared experiences
2026
Chapter 07 / The brothers reunite

TCRS by VIBE CITY — Rise again

In 2026, BlackedOut arrives in TCRS by VIBE CITY for a fresh beginning. After years of memories and different journeys across roleplay cities, the brothers have another chance to reunite.

The mission is bigger than simply returning: rebuild BLACK GANG, establish its identity, and begin the journey from the ground up.

  • Brothers reunite in a new city
  • A new identity and reputation to build
  • New opportunities, businesses, and memories ahead
